# 0.4.2 — 2026-10-24

## Added

- **`openmeteo_get_forecast` `models`** ([#80](https://github.com/cyanheads/open-meteo-mcp-server/issues/80)) — compares deterministic models side by side from a documented list of 57; with 2+ models every hourly and daily column and unit key carries a model suffix, and the output echoes `models`. A model Open-Meteo drops for lacking data at the coordinate is named in the `notice`. `current_variables` with 2+ models fails as `current_multi_model`.
- **`openmeteo_get_flood` `models`** ([#79](https://github.com/cyanheads/open-meteo-mcp-server/issues/79)) — selects `seamless_v4` (the default), `consolidated_v4`, or `forecast_v4`; the output echoes `models`, and the `format()` heading names the model.
- **`openmeteo_get_marine` `current_variables`, `length_unit`, `wind_speed_unit`, `temperature_unit`** ([#81](https://github.com/cyanheads/open-meteo-mcp-server/issues/81)) — `current_variables` returns a `current` block with `current_units`, alone or beside either window; the unit inputs convert heights to ft, ocean current speed to `mph`, `ms`, or `kn`, and sea surface temperature to °F. A daily-only name in `current_variables` fails as `variable_wrong_cadence`.
- **`openmeteo_get_elevation` `unprocessed_count` and `next_offset`** ([#82](https://github.com/cyanheads/open-meteo-mcp-server/issues/82)) — `latitudes` and `longitudes` accept up to 1,000 pairs and the first 100 are sampled per call; resubmit `latitudes.slice(next_offset)` and `longitudes.slice(next_offset)` until `next_offset` is `null`.

## Changed

- **DataCanvas is on by default**: `CANVAS_PROVIDER_TYPE` defaults to `duckdb`, so oversized results stage on a canvas and the dataframe tools work without configuration. `none` disables it, and a blank or unsubstituted `${…}` value counts as unset. The `.mcpb` bundle pins `none` and no longer offers the canvas and dataframe-drop toggles.
- **Service failures carry a declared `data.reason`** ([#77](https://github.com/cyanheads/open-meteo-mcp-server/issues/77)): `coverage_gap` (declared on the forecast, ensemble, historical, marine, and flood tools), `upstream_unavailable`, and `upstream_timeout` (declared on all nine upstream tools), each with a recovery hint. Codes and messages are unchanged.
- **`openmeteo_get_flood` descriptions** ([#79](https://github.com/cyanheads/open-meteo-mcp-server/issues/79)) — an omitted `models` reads `seamless_v4`: `consolidated_v4` reanalysis before 2023-08-05, forecast-run data from then on. `consolidated_v4` reaches reanalysis through 2025-05-31, and the ensemble statistics carry spread only on forecast-run days.
- **`openmeteo_dataframe_query` `sql` description** ([#78](https://github.com/cyanheads/open-meteo-mcp-server/issues/78)) — states that `time`, `sunrise`, and `sunset` are ISO 8601 text, casts `time` in the monthly-mean example, and adds a member-aware `openmeteo_get_ensemble` example.
- **`invalid_variable` hints** on the forecast, flood, historical, and climate tools list the documented models only when the call sent `models`.
- **Marine `no_variables_requested` and `request_too_large`** messages name `current_variables`.
- Docs: `README.md` and `docs/design.md` describe the new inputs and the canvas default.

## Fixed

- **`openmeteo_get_historical`** names a model the archive drops from a multi-model request, such as `cerra` beside `era5` outside Europe, or says the plain-named columns cannot be attributed to a model ([#83](https://github.com/cyanheads/open-meteo-mcp-server/issues/83)).
- **A timeout while reading the response body** is retried and surfaces as `upstream_timeout`, where it escaped the retry ladder unclassified ([#77](https://github.com/cyanheads/open-meteo-mcp-server/issues/77)).
